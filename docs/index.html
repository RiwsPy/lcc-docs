Have you ever wondered about the mystery of the Ice Island that Shandalar sends you to? Chronicles of the Sword Coast is a component-based quest mod that adds numerous small quests to the game, alongside miscellaneous components introducing new mechanics, items, and interjections from BioWare and Beamdog characters. Alexia Kalila Johara Al-Mustafa or X as she has been known to go by, claims she's from the city of Calimport from the country of Calimshan.

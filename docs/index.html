Sandrah Saga I - Sandrah Appears
⚠️✅😀

EET

Roxanne

This is a huge project designed for EET. If you like Mary Sue, then this is the mod for you. A young fighter-priestess of Mystra, originally from Waterdeep, Sandrah is the daughter of a famous figure in the Kingdoms, who emerges from her father's powerful shadow to make a name for herself and also to discover the hidden part of her family history. The plot unfolds as the PC discovers who he/she is. But make no mistake, your character's story takes a back seat to Sandrah's! And finally, the icing on the cake! Sandrah Saga requires a full installation of EET with all the "big mods". Or more precisely, the version of the mega mods whose scenarios have been modified by the author to suit her heroine's story. In short, a megalomaniac mod in the extreme...

EET

IWDEE

Deratiseur

Urkodoël is an anarchilich whom you can find in Athkatla's Graveyard, in the dead end beside Uncle Lester (BG2EE), or in the small cave in the lower-left corner of Kuldahar Pass (IWDEE).
He can teach arcane spells to any cleric, druid, or shaman (including multiclass characters). This costs no gold. Instead, it permanently reduces maximum hit points by 1 per spell level.

Urkodoël also sells equipment, including three amulets that can alter how his services work.

Oops is a very special character: he has the ability to change a spell scroll into a special ability. AutoDescription is a mod, for BG2, BGT, BGEE, BG2EE and EET, developed on La Couronne de Cuivre, which automatically generates and replaces the "technical" description of items and spells.

This has several advantages:
- Descriptions are standardized, making them easier to read- The description adapts to any modifications made by mods and harmonizes them with the rest of the game- By comparing before-and-after descriptions, we can detect any bugs, errors or incompatibilities between mods and the base game.
- If untranslated mods are used, the description is automatically updated. If untranslated mods are installed, the item statistics of these mods will be automatically translated and harmonized.

Initially, this mod was designed to be used by players, being installed after all mods adding or altering items.

In fact, AutoDescription adapts to all mods installed by the user, so if the capabilities of certain items are altered, these will be updated so that their description corresponds to reality and in your original language.

In practice, it turns out that AutoDescription can also be used in mod development, at various levels...

For translators, it automatically translates the statistics contained in the descriptions of most items, thus avoiding an often tedious and redundant task.

For developers, it can highlight certain problems, inconsistencies or bugs in items by reliably translating the effects. To this end, a number of errors and warnings are logged. As the mod was not originally designed for this purpose, the list of errors reported in this way is still small, but will grow over time.

Verr’Sza is a rakshasa hunter of bad alignment who can be recruited at the Friendly Arm inn. The mod is compatible with Siege of Dragonspear and features content specific to that expansion, as well as related to the Sirene NPC for BG:EE mod.
The mod includes some go-and-kill quests, as well as a romance for both male and female characters (the only condition is not being of Lawful Good alignment).
